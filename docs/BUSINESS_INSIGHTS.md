# Business Insights

## Objective

Analyze sales performance, profitability, product categories, payment methods, and monthly trends using the Power BI dashboard.

## Key Findings

Add three to five findings supported by the actual dataset and dashboard.

For each finding, document:

1. **Observation:** What does the data show?
2. **Evidence:** What metric or comparison supports it?
3. **Business implication:** Why does it matter?
4. **Recommendation:** What action could be considered?

## Data Limitations

Document missing data, assumptions, excluded orders, and any limitations that affect interpretation.

*Do not publish unverified findings or recommendations as established facts.*
