# Business Insights — Amazon India Sales Analysis

## 1. Executive Summary

The dataset contains 10,000 unique orders across five product categories, multiple payment methods, fulfillment channels, and Indian states.

The analysis focuses on sales performance, profitability, customer payment preferences, product contributions, geographic distribution, and order outcomes.

### Key Performance Indicators

| KPI | Result |
|---|---:|
| Total Sales | ₹155.79 million |
| Total Profit | ₹33.17 million |
| Profit Margin | 21.29% |
| Total Orders | 10,000 |
| Total Units Sold | 24,926 |
| Average Sales per Order | ₹15,578.99 |

*These totals are calculated from the supplied Excel dataset. Sales and profit totals reflect the recorded data, not necessarily realized revenue after returns or cancellations.*

## 2. Key Findings

### Finding 1: Electronics & Mobiles Leads Sales

Electronics & Mobiles generated approximately ₹123.57 million in recorded sales, representing around 79.32% of total sales.

Its recorded profit was approximately ₹26.31 million.

**Business implication:** Sales are heavily concentrated in one product category.

**Recommendation:** Investigate the drivers of this category's performance and monitor its contribution to overall profitability and business risk.

### Finding 2: UPI Is the Leading Payment Method

UPI accounted for approximately ₹78.52 million in sales, or 50.40% of total recorded sales.

Credit/Debit Card ranked second, contributing approximately ₹31.07 million.

**Business implication:** UPI is the largest payment channel in this dataset.

**Recommendation:** Maintain a smooth UPI payment experience and monitor transaction failures and payment-related cancellations.

### Finding 3: Maharashtra Leads State-Level Sales

Maharashtra recorded approximately ₹29.55 million in sales, followed by Karnataka at ₹24.42 million and Delhi at ₹22.36 million.

**Business implication:** Recorded sales are concentrated in a small number of leading states.

**Recommendation:** Compare regional demand, fulfillment performance, and profitability before making location-specific investment decisions.

### Finding 4: Amazon FBA Is the Largest Fulfillment Channel

Amazon (FBA) recorded approximately ₹107.53 million in sales, representing around 69.01% of total recorded sales.

Its recorded profit was approximately ₹22.88 million.

**Business implication:** FBA is the dominant fulfillment channel in the dataset.

**Recommendation:** Compare fulfillment costs, delivery performance, cancellation rates, and profit margins across all fulfillment methods before drawing conclusions about operational efficiency.

### Finding 5: Order Status Requires Further Analysis

The dataset contains the following order statuses:

| Order Status | Orders | Percentage |
|---|---:|---:|
| Delivered | 8,147 | 81.47% |
| Shipped | 868 | 8.68% |
| Cancelled | 500 | 5.00% |
| Returned | 485 | 4.85% |

**Business implication:** Cancelled and returned orders together represent 9.85% of recorded orders.

**Recommendation:** Investigate cancellation and return reasons, and determine their impact on realized revenue and net profitability.

## 3. Data Limitations

- The dataset does not establish whether its records represent real Amazon transactions or a sample dataset.
- Recorded sales and profit may include cancelled or returned orders.
- The available fields do not provide customer demographics or explicit cancellation reasons.
- The calculated profit margin is based on recorded profit divided by recorded sales; it should not automatically be interpreted as net margin.
- Findings describe this dataset and should not be generalized to all Amazon India sales.

## 4. Conclusion

The analysis highlights the concentration of sales in Electronics & Mobiles, the leading role of UPI, regional sales differences, and the dominance of Amazon FBA.

Further analysis of order outcomes, fulfillment costs, and realized profitability would provide a more complete view of business performance.
